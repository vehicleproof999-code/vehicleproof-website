Common questions

How do I start an inspection?

Open a vehicle and tap New Inspection, choose the type (check-in, check-out, routine or pre-purchase), and follow the camera around the vehicle one angle at a time.

Can I compare two inspections?

Yes. Once a vehicle has a finished check-in and check-out, open Compare Photos to see each angle side by side or with a slider.

A scanned receipt has a wrong amount or date.

Scanning is automatic and can misread some receipts. Correct the fields before you save; you can also edit the record later.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account, which works in the app or by email.
