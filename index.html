Everything about your vehicle, in one place

Built for owners, families and small fleets who want records that hold up when it matters: a rental return, a resale or an insurance claim.

Photo-proof inspections

A guided camera walks you around the vehicle, angle by angle. Each photo is fingerprinted and the finished report is sealed.

See what changed

Compare a check-in and a check-out side by side, or with a slider, and spot new scratches and dents straight away.

Service history

Log services and repairs, or scan the workshop receipt and let the app read the details for you.

Documents

Insurance, road tax and registration papers, kept with the vehicle they belong to, with their expiry dates.

Renewal reminders

Get a heads-up before road tax, insurance or the next service is due, so nothing lapses by surprise.

Private by design

Your records are stored privately, and you can keep documents in your own Google Drive. Delete your account at any time.

Proof for the moments that matter

Handing over a rental car, selling your vehicle or making a claim goes more smoothly when the evidence is already organised.

  • Check-in and check-out inspections with timestamped photos
  • Damage noted on the exact panel, with severity
  • A shareable report of every inspection
  • Service, expense and document history in one timeline
