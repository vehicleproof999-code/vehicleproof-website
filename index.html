Inspections

Walk around. Tap.
Proven.

A guided inspection records the condition of any vehicle in a few minutes, with evidence that's hard to argue with.

01 · Start

Pick the moment.

A check-in before a rental or handover. A check-out when it comes back. A routine check, or a close look at a car you're about to buy.

02 · Capture

Every angle, guided.

The camera walks you around the vehicle one angle at a time, with zoom and your progress always in view. Nothing gets missed.

03 · Review

Condition at a glance.

Issues are pinned to the exact spot on the car, next to every angle you've captured, and any that are still missing.

04 · Compare

Spot what changed.

Put a check-in next to a check-out and drag the slider. New scratches and dents stand out straight away.
